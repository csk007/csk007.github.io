Dress Code Policies
  • Mandatory ID Cards:

    Must be worn daily by students of Classes I to XI, for external events and examinations.

  • Saturday Events:

    Wear Wednesday's White T-Shirt uniform for PTMs/events.

  • Girls Dress Rules:

    Must wear tights under uniforms & slips over shirts. No colourful jackets, rings, earrings, bangles, mehndi, or nail polish.

  • Boys Dress Rules:

    No colourful jackets or unusual hairstyles. Khada (silver bangle) is allowed only for Sikh boys (must not cause harm). No jewelry.

  • Winter Wear:

    Only school-prescribed sweaters allowed. Must be pre-ordered in May. No hoodies or alternative jackets.

  • Yoga Attire:

    Only school-prescribed yoga pants allowed (must change after class). Permitted under uniform Dec-Jan.

  • Watches & Caps:

    Only analog watches are allowed; no expensive/digital watches. Only school-prescribed dark blue synthetic caps are allowed for travel (remove upon entry).

  • Birthdays & Gifts:

    There should be no exchange of gifts for birthdays and friendship days in the school.
